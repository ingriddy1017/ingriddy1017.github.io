About
Economics, math, and data in service of real questions and real research

I'm a student at the University of Maryland studying economics, mathematics, and data science. My interests range from socioeconomic issues and public policy to finance and business — I like the questions that don't have a clean textbook answer yet.

Outside the notebook, I build things: county-level housing models, listening-pattern pipelines, and a handful of side projects driven by music and plain curiosity.

04

Single-Family Zoning & the Housing Crisis: A Data Analysis

A companion data pipeline to my presentation on ending single-family zoning. Charts a century of rising residential segregation alongside a Minneapolis case study, comparing housing approvals and rent growth against five peer Midwestern metros after the city ended single-family-only zoning in 2019.
